# Problem Solver

Meet **Bob**, the robot who lives under the main menu.

## Problem Bob
Press the **blue robot button** next to the Geode button on the main menu. Bob bursts out of the ground (he's very glad to be free) and offers his help:

- **Bug Fixes**: the bugs Bob has patched. When he fixes a new one, he pops up on the main menu to tell you about it.
- **Level Help**: tips from Level Beater Bob.
- **Editor Guide**: opens Buster Bob.
- **Settings**

## Buster Bob (editor)
- **Mobile:** tap the pause button (**||**) in the editor, then the blue robot button.
- **PC:** press **F** in the editor (you can change the key in the settings).

Pick a style, a quality (High / Medium / Low) and a detail level (High / Medium / Low). Buster Bob then walks you through the whole level: song, layout, colors (with color codes to copy), blocks, decoration, effects, optimizing, and verifying. He remembers which step you're on, so you can close him, build, and come back.

## Level Beater Bob
While you play, Bob counts where you die. When one part keeps getting you, he slides in with a tip, and he cheers when you set a new best. He never plays for you, so your runs stay legit.
